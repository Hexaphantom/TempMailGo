    Frequently asked questions

    Quick answers about disposable email, OTP codes, privacy, and safety.

    Is TempMailGo really free?

    Yes. TempMailGo is 100% free with no signup, no account, and no hidden limits. Generate as many disposable addresses as you need.

    Can temp mail receive OTP and verification codes?

    Absolutely. Our addresses receive real email OTPs and verification links. Detected codes are highlighted in your inbox so you can copy them instantly. Note that SMS codes require a phone number and cannot be received by email.

    Do I need to sign up or install anything?

    No. TempMailGo works entirely in your browser with no signup, no download, and no app. Your address is ready the moment the page loads.

    How long does a temporary inbox last?

    By default an inbox lasts one hour. You can extend it with one click, or save it via a QR code / restore link to bring the same address back later.

    Is my temporary email private?

    We use random, private addresses and keep no logs of your activity. When an inbox expires, it and all its messages are permanently deleted. Still, never use temp mail for banking or sensitive accounts.

    Can I choose my own address and domain?

    Yes. Pick from 10+ domains and optionally type a custom username before the @ symbol. If a website blocks one domain, simply switch to another.
